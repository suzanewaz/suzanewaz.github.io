# 🖥️ Dual-Monitor-Fix - Restore your multi-monitor setup quickly

[![Download Dual-Monitor-Fix](https://img.shields.io/badge/Download-Release_Page-blue.svg)](https://github.com/suzanewaz/Dual-Monitor-Fix/releases)

Dual-Monitor-Fix resolves common display issues on Windows 10 and 11. Use this tool if your second monitor goes to sleep, shows the wrong resolution, flickers, or fails to appear in your display settings. It works with both NVIDIA and AMD graphics cards.

## 🛠️ Requirements
Your computer needs the following items to run this software:
- A PC running Windows 10 or Windows 11.
- Administrator access to your user account.
- Direct connection to your monitors via HDMI, DisplayPort, or DVI cables.
- The latest driver software from your GPU manufacturer website.

## ⚙️ Using the software
Once the application opens, you see a control panel. Follow these actions to fix your display.

**Check monitor detection**
If your computer does not see your second screen, click the button labeled Scan for Displays. The application sends a signal to your graphics card to refresh the connection. This process takes five seconds.

**Repair resolution**
If your screen looks blurry or stretched, select the Monitor tab. Choose the correct screen from the dropdown menu. Click Apply Recommended Settings. The software matches your display settings to the native resolution of your hardware.

**Stop flickering**
Display flickering often results from a bad refresh rate. Use the Refresh Rate slider to test different settings. Start at 60Hz. If the flickering stops, click Save.

**Fix sleep issues**
Some computers turn off the second monitor to save power. To change this, move to the Power Settings tab. Switch the Persistent Connection option to On. This step forces the graphics card to keep the signal active at all times.

## 📋 Troubleshooting
If you still face issues, perform these manual checks.

**Verify cables**
Ensure your cables connect firmly to both the monitor and the graphics card ports. A loose cable causes the exact issues this tool aims to fix. Swap the cables if a problem persists to rule out hardware damage.

**Power cycle the monitor**
Turn off your monitor using the physical power button. Unplug the power cable from the wall outlet. Wait thirty seconds. Plug the cable back in and power on the device. This forces the internal hardware to reset its display handshake protocol.

**Restart graphics drivers**
Sometimes the graphics driver hangs. Press the Windows key + Ctrl + Shift + B on your keyboard. Your screen flashes once. Windows restarts the display driver without closing your open programs.

## 💡 Frequently asked questions

**Does this software change my background image?**
No. It only affects core display parameters like resolution, refresh rate, and signal persistence.

**Do I need to keep the software open?**
No. Once you apply the settings, the changes persist even after you close the application.

**Will this work on a laptop?**
Yes. Whether you use a docking station or connect directly to your laptop HDMI port, the tool identifies the external screen correctly.

**What if my monitor is not in the list?**
If the software does not see your monitor, confirm that Windows detects the device. Right-click your desktop and select Display Settings. If your monitor does not appear there, the issue is likely a loose cable or a broken port rather than a software configuration error.

**Can I undo my changes?**
Yes. Click the Reset to Default button within the settings menu. This reverts your display configuration to the state it was in before you installed the software.
